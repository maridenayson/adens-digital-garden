## ✿ what is a digital garden?

According to Margaret Alba's video, *"Digital Gardening 101"*, a digital garden is like your personal "wiki page". It's a place where you log all your interests, thoughts, collections, discoveries, and all other things you love. If you feel like you're the type to constantly go down internet rabbit holes or you just have **way** too many interests to keep track of; a digital garden might be very helpful for you. 

After watching Margaret Alba's video (the one linked below), I immediately decided that I would learn to code despite not having any previous coding knowledge (aside from Tumblr) just so I could make my own digital garden. With the help of multiple programs like **Obsidian**, **Visual Studio Code**, and **Github**, along with copious amounts of research, I was able to make my very own personalized *"digital garden"*. 

Feel free to explore :)
